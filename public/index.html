Transparency and provenance

This site is static and read-only. It does not request forecasts, models, databases, or AI services.

About / Data Sources Texas Energy Intelligence is an independent experimental AI/ML portfolio project demonstrating short-term ERCOT electricity-demand forecasting. Predictions use historical electricity demand and weather forecasts; results are experimental and are not intended for grid dispatch, electricity trading, or safety-critical decisions. Forecast values are estimates, source availability can lag, and missing observations remain pending rather than being inferred or treated as zero. Forecast methods may improve through documented experiments, but any candidate remains subject to evaluation and explicit approval before production use.

Demand observations are sourced from the U.S. Energy Information Administration (EIA) for ERCOT ERCO/D, not directly from ERCOT. Context: ERCOT. Weather data are provided through Open-Meteo under CC BY 4.0, using ECMWF IFS issued forecasts where labelled; see ECMWF. This project is independently developed and is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by ERCOT, EIA, Open-Meteo, or ECMWF.
